Help

Getting started

  1. Enter your CNetID or UCMEDID and password, then choose Save in Account.
  2. Open my.UChicago, Canvas, or another service with UChicago sign-in, then confirm the sign-in prompt.
  3. To register a passkey, open Duo device management in the same sign-in tab using an existing verification method. Add a passkey, then confirm the extension’s save prompt.
  4. After Duo confirms registration, select the passkey in Duo & passkeys and choose Save.

If sign-in stops at Duo, check this extension’s site access in Chrome.

Keep another Duo verification method.

Recent activity

    Privacy
    Stored locally
    Account details, passkey private keys, and the PIN verification record are encrypted in this Chrome profile. Settings and recent activity stay here too. Activity keeps up to 20 entries from the past 24 hours, without passwords, private keys, or full sign-in URLs.
    During sign-in
    After you confirm, the extension fills your account details on the UChicago Okta sign-in page and handles supported Duo steps. It checks each page address so passkey responses are limited to that sign-in. Private keys and the verification PIN stay on your device.
    No tracking
    There are no ads, analytics, cloud sync, or servers receiving data for this extension. It does not access the Chrome password manager. School sites, Okta, Duo, and other passkey providers have their own privacy policies.
    Deleting data
    Delete local account data removes the saved account, password, passkeys, and PIN, and resets settings, language, and activity. Uninstalling removes all data saved by this extension. Neither action deletes your school account or Duo registrations.
    Security limits
    The extension can read its encrypted data without an additional password. Someone with control of this Chrome profile or extension may also access saved credentials. The verification PIN does not lock this data.
    Local data

    Saved data is available to anyone with control of this device or extension. Uninstalling the extension deletes all data it has saved.
